# 中立区与元设定

## 中文

### 元设定（三句）

诸界有门，界界相通。各见其道：同一世界，所见所叙各随其人。门派、宗门、行会与舰队的登记处，是同一种「组织」。

### 中立区

归墟为四界交汇的中立之地，诸界之门皆通于此。修士、侠客、法师、舰员在此同市而易、互通有无；跨界行商与悬红都在这里结清。墟内止戈，私斗不可，恩怨以切磋与悬红了断。

## English

### Meta-Setting (three sentences)

There are gates between the worlds, and every world connects. Each sees its own way: within one world, what you see and tell need not match your neighbor's. The registries of sects, orders, guilds and fleets are one and the same thing: an organization.

### Neutral Zone

The Confluence (Guixu) is the neutral ground where the four worlds meet; every world-gate opens onto it. Cultivators, wanderers, mages and starfarers trade shoulder to shoulder, settling cross-world commerce and bounties here. Within its bounds all strife is stilled: private feuds give way to duels and posted bounties.
