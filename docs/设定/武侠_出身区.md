# 武侠出身区

## 中文

**出身区：**青溪镇临山傍水，江湖行籍所为默认登记组织；镇旁松影木人巷是入门首关。

**组织命名：**
- 江湖行籍所：登记江湖中人的往来名籍。
- 青溪镖局：以护送行旅与货物为营生的江湖组织。
- 松风武馆：在镇边传习武艺的武馆。
- 听雨药社：采集与研习草药的同道所聚之处。

## English

**Origin region:** Qingxi Town lies between hills and water. The Jianghu Registry is its default registration organization; the Pine-Shadow Wooden Dummy Lane beside town is the introductory challenge.

**Organizations:**
- Jianghu Registry: Keeps the registers of those who travel the martial world.
- Qingxi Escort Agency: A martial-world organization that escorts travelers and goods.
- Pinewind Martial Hall: A school teaching martial arts on the edge of town.
- Listening Rain Herb Society: A gathering of people who collect and study herbs.
