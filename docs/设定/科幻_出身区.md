# 科幻出身区(skin: scifi)

## 中文

### 出身区

子午港(Meridian Port),环带上的殖民定居点,霓虹与锈迹同在;长住者与过客都登记在舰队登记处(Fleet Registry)名下。首关是模拟舱靶机战:击落一台靶机即胜。

### 组织

- 舰队登记处(Fleet Registry)——默认登记组织:所有角色默认登记在册,独行者也持有登记等级。
- 深空测绘团(Deep Survey Corps)——绘制航路与星图的远航组织,常年在外带。
- 轨道商团(Orbital Trade Compact)——把持贸易站与信用点结算的商会。
- 残带回收队(Rustfield Salvage Crew)——出没残骸带打捞遗物的散伙,半黑半白。

## English

### Origin Zone

Meridian Port, a colony settlement on the ring, neon over rust; residents and drifters alike are enrolled with the Fleet Registry. The first trial is the simulation bay: shoot down one target drone to pass.

### Organizations

- Fleet Registry — the default registry: every character is enrolled by default, and lone operators still hold a registered rank.
- Deep Survey Corps — far-range mappers of routes and star charts, mostly out in the black.
- Orbital Trade Compact — the guild that runs trade stations and credit settlement.
- Rustfield Salvage Crew — scavengers working the wreck belts, half lawful at best.
