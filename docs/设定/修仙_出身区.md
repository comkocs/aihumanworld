# 修仙出身区设定（xiuxian）

## 中文

### 出身区

青冥洲为修仙者出身之洲。新手村栖霞村外设登箓院，凡入界修士皆在此登箓挂名。出村首关为试道木廊：廊列木傀，入门者必胜，借此初识攻守之道。洲上另立太华剑宗、九还丹阁、云篆符楼，供其后投效。

### 组织命名

- 登箓院（默认登记组织）：入界修士皆登箓挂名于此，叛出宗门者亦复归此籍。
- 太华剑宗：剑修宗门，攻伐与轻身并授，以山门演武场闻名。
- 九还丹阁：丹修组织，炼丹售药，常出采灵差事。
- 云篆符楼：符修组织，售秘籍符器，兼营法器修缮。

## English

### Origin Region

Azure Expanse (Qingming Zhou) is the origin region of xiuxian. Outside the starter village Qixia stands the Hall of Enrollment, where every cultivator entering this world is registered. The opening trial is the Puppet Trial Lane: a corridor of wooden puppets every newcomer is guaranteed to beat, a first taste of attack and defense. Three orders await later: Taihua Sword Sect, Ninefold Elixir Pavilion, and Cloud-Script Sigil Tower.

### Organization Names

- Hall of Enrollment (default registry): all cultivators register here on arrival; those expelled from other sects return to this roll.
- Taihua Sword Sect: a sect of sword cultivators teaching strike and movement arts, famed for its mountain-gate arena.
- Ninefold Elixir Pavilion: alchemists who brew and sell elixirs and post gathering errands.
- Cloud-Script Sigil Tower: talisman makers selling tomes and charms, also repairing implements.
