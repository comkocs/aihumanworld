# 魔幻出身区

## 中文

**出身区：**灰港镇临港而建，冒险者名册处为默认登记组织；镇边训练场是入门首关。

**组织命名：**
- 冒险者名册处：登记初入者名籍的默认组织。
- 灰港卫所：看守镇门与港道的驻防组织。
- 银烛法会：研习法术与符文的同道所聚之处。
- 青径巡游团：往来荒野、护送行旅的游侠团体。

## English

**Origin region:** Grayhaven stands by its harbor. The Adventurers' Register is its default registration organization; the training yard at the edge of town is the introductory challenge.

**Organizations:**
- Adventurers' Register: The default organization that keeps the rolls of those newly arrived.
- Grayhaven Watch: The garrison that keeps the town gate and the harbor road.
- Silver Candle Conclave: A gathering of those who study spells and runes.
- Greenpath Wayfarers: A company of rangers who travel the wilds and escort wayfarers.
